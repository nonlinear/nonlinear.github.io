Delay Tolerant for AI

What store-and-forward networks teach about running models where the signal is intermittent — and why the constraint is the design.

The first thing to notice is that the constraint is not an obstacle to the design — it is the design. Everything that works here works because something else was ruled out first.

The shape of the problem

We tend to describe this as a gap to be closed. Framed that way, every answer looks like more: more bandwidth, more storage, more computation. The other framing asks what becomes possible when the gap is permanent and acknowledged.

What changes

Once the gap is treated as given, the interesting decisions move earlier. You stop asking how to move the data and start asking what has to be true before the data is worth moving at all.

Where this leaves us

Not with a solution, but with a better question. That is usually the honest place to stop.
